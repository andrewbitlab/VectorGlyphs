# Business Plan — VectorGlyphs

## Business thesis

VectorGlyphs can be a focused micro-product: a fast, credible vector glyph generator that monetizes impulse purchases and grows through SEO plus generated visual marketing assets.

## Initial positioning

**Vector Glyph Generator — Create SVG & PNG glyphs for apps, UI, logos and digital products.**

The product should emphasize procedural/vector/design-tool value rather than generic AI image generation.

## Pricing hypothesis

Initial:

- Single glyph export: `$1`

Includes:

- Clean SVG
- Selected PNG size
- Commercial-use license

Near-term upsell:

- Full export pack: `$2`

Includes:

- SVG
- PNG 512
- PNG 1024
- PNG 2048
- PNG 4096
- `manifest.json`
- `license.txt`

Later tests:

- 10 glyph credits: `$5`
- 30 glyph credits: `$12`
- Brand glyph pack: `$19-$49`
- Custom consistent glyph collection: `$49+`

## Margin note

A `$1` price is good for impulse conversion but payment processor fees will be proportionally high. Use it as entry pricing, then test packs/credits for better margin.

## Acquisition strategy

Primary channels:

- Google SEO
- Product Hunt
- X/Twitter
- Indie Hackers
- Hacker News Show HN
- Carefully selected Reddit communities
- Design inspiration platforms
- Directories for design/dev tools

Designer platforms to evaluate:

- Dribbble
- Behance
- Pinterest
- Figma Community
- Layers.to
- UI8/Gumroad packs later
- Uneed
- BetaList
- SaaSHub
- There’s An AI For That only if AI positioning remains truthful and acceptable

## Growth loop

1. Generate high-quality glyphs and UI inspiration visuals.
2. Publish or queue approved visuals across safe platforms.
3. Drive traffic to `vectorglyphs.com`.
4. Convert free previews into paid exports.
5. Use purchased/exported examples and feedback to refine styles, SEO pages, and marketing themes.

## Key risks

- Low conversion at `$1` due to payment friction.
- Overclaiming uniqueness or logo/trademark suitability.
- Generic AI-art perception.
- Platform automation risks for visual marketing.
- Premature infrastructure complexity.

## Initial success signals

- Visitors generate multiple glyphs per session.
- Users click download/export before payment is live.
- Feedback identifies clear use cases.
- SEO pages start attracting design/dev intent traffic.
- First paid downloads validate willingness to pay.
